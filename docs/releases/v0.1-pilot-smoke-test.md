## Procedure and acceptance

1. Invite and provision the synthetic CUSTOMER Organization. Verify status ACTIVE, zero activity/report rows, demo factors disabled, and no Golden Sample data.
2. Login through the customer wording. Verify Sample Data is hidden and platform navigation/direct routes are denied.
3. Verify Add data presents only Upload documents, Import spreadsheet, and Manual entry. In Upload documents, reject CSV/XLS/XLSX with an actionable redirect to Import spreadsheet; accept only PDF/JPG/JPEG/PNG.
4. Import the dataset through Import spreadsheet, review all rows, and confirm only the seven ready rows. Leave the missing-province row unresolved.
5. In Data Records, verify the seven calculated records and one unresolved review row have the expected customer-friendly statuses. The unresolved row must not appear calculated or as a zero-emission included row.
6. Verify Natural Gas GJ and m3 select distinct reviewed factor identities; Hotel is six room-nights and appears exactly once; Air Travel uses the structured route, passenger count, server-derived passenger-km, and reviewed haul factor.
7. Verify Dashboard and Calculation results show location-based Scope 2 disclosure and no market-based zero.
8. Open Formal Report and reconcile the canonical aggregate, scope/category breakdown, source evidence, and exclusion count with Dashboard.
9. Export CSV and PDF. Require Dashboard, Formal Report, CSV, and PDF totals to agree exactly and the unresolved row to remain excluded.
10. Open Methodology & emission factors as the customer. Require read-only production-approved entries and customer-safe metadata only; no Factor Library administration.
11. Upload a synthetic supported source document, verify extraction/review, download the authenticated original, and compare its bytes or SHA-256.
12. Verify an Organization ADMIN still cannot access factor administration, cross-Organization activity, provisioning, erasure, or other platform-admin tools.
13. Erase the synthetic Organization through the supported platform workflow. Verify its database rows and stored sources are removed according to the erasure result.

Overall smoke is PASS only if every step passes and the exact deployed SHA pair is rechecked. Record sanitized evidence references; never commit credentials, tokens, connection strings, raw browser traces, or source documents.

## Abort conditions

Abort rollout if a migration fails; the factor gate is not PASS or not exactly 21/21/21; database/readiness/storage health fails; upload/download integrity fails; tenant isolation or platform/customer-admin separation fails; Golden Sample data appears; any unresolved row is included or treated as zero; Hotel is double-counted; or Dashboard, Formal Report, CSV, and PDF totals disagree.

Keep first-customer onboarding disabled. Follow the validated paired backup/recovery runbook and redeploy the previously recorded SHA pair. Do not use manual SQL as an improvised recovery step.
