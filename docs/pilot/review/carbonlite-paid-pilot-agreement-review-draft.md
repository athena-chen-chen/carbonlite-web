## 1. Parties and effect

This Controlled Paid Pilot Agreement (the **Agreement**) is between **Kach Canada Ltd.**, an Alberta corporation operating CarbonLite (**Kach**), and **[Customer legal name and address]** (**Customer**). CarbonLite is a service name, not a separate legal entity. The Agreement takes effect only after both authorized representatives sign and date it.

This draft does not authorize real customer data. Kach must separately record closure of all applicable real-data P0 gates and identify the approved environment before Customer uploads real data.

## 2. Purpose and excluded services

The pilot evaluates workflow fit, data readiness, factor matching, calculation provenance, review, and draft reporting for a bounded dataset. It is not a production migration, regulatory filing service, assurance engagement, certification, audit, verification, carbon-credit eligibility determination, or professional legal, accounting, engineering, environmental, or regulatory advice.

## 4. Customer responsibilities

Customer must have authority to provide the data, submit only approved and necessary data, maintain originals and its authoritative accounting archive, administer its named users, review every extraction/mapping/factor/calculation/exclusion/export, and obtain professional advice for formal use. Prohibited data categories in the retention policy are incorporated into Schedule B.

## 5. AI-assisted extraction

AI is off for real customer data unless the signed schedule identifies the provider, purpose, data, region, retention/abuse-monitoring treatment, contractual safeguards, and customer authorization. AI output requires human review and may be incomplete or wrong. `store: false` or similar technical settings are not immediate-deletion guarantees. A spreadsheet/manual workflow remains available when AI is not authorized.

## 6. Factors, methodology, and reliance

CarbonLite calculates only records that pass approved eligibility rules and have an approved factor with the required unit, geography, period, context, and provenance. Unsupported or incomplete rows are marked for review or not calculated and are excluded from totals.

Where supported, electricity reporting is location-based Scope 2. Market-based Scope 2 is not calculated and must not be interpreted as zero. Outputs do not guarantee completeness, accuracy, regulatory compliance, acceptance, assurance, verification, or certification.

## 7. Data ownership and processing rights

Customer retains its rights in Customer data. Customer grants Kach a limited right during the Agreement and approved retention period to host, copy, transmit, structure, calculate, display, support, secure, back up, export, and delete that data only to perform the pilot, comply with law, resolve incidents/disputes, and follow an authorized Legal Hold.

Qualified counsel must determine the parties' privacy roles and whether a DPA is required.

## 8. Privacy, subprocessors, and cross-border processing

The signed Schedule B must list only verified providers and state their purpose, data classes, processing/storage locations, retention/deletion terms, safeguards, DPA status, and material-change process. Some processing may occur outside Canada and be subject to foreign law; exact locations and appropriate disclosure remain pending legal/infrastructure review.

## 9. Retention, deletion, Legal Holds, and offboarding

The proposed source-document rule is active-system deletion at pilot end + 30 calendar days, subject to holds, contract exceptions, export, verified execution, quarantine, and backup disclosure. Confirmed records are contract-defined; Customer is proposed as the authoritative archive.

Persistent Legal Holds and guarded deletion exist locally, but they are not production approved. Hold release does not delete data. Source deletion may create a recoverable quarantine copy; purge is a separate exact-ID reviewed action. Workspace erasure does not technically enforce an independently signed second-person approval bound to the exact operation.

Proposed offboarding gives Customer 14 days to export and targets active-system completion within 30 days after the later approved trigger, export window, and hold/contract clearance. Access termination, active-system deletion, quarantine purge, provider deletion, backup expiry, and evidence expiry are separate milestones.

## 10. Confidentiality and security

Each party will protect the other's non-public information with reasonable care and use it only for the pilot, subject to approved personnel, professional advisers, providers, and lawful disclosure. Counsel must review exclusions, duration, remedies, and compelled-disclosure wording.

Kach promises only controls actually listed in the signed schedule and evidenced for the approved environment. No certification, penetration-test result, encryption configuration, or security framework conformance is implied.

## 11. Availability, incidents, backup, and recovery

The pilot is provided as available with no uptime, response, resolution, continuous-availability, or recovery guarantee. Customer must retain originals and exports.

Incident notice threshold, authority, channel, cadence, and timing are pending legal/security review. Internal backup proposals (daily paired capture, 14-day rotation, 24-hour RPO, maximum 30-day deleted-data survival) are not contractual until provider, restore, and expiry evidence exists. No RTO is approved.

## 12. Intellectual property and feedback

Kach retains CarbonLite software, branding, interfaces, templates, documentation, and general methods. Customer retains Customer data and confidential information. Third-party factors, software, and publications remain subject to their own rights.

Any feedback licence must exclude Customer confidential information, personal information, sources, and identifying facts. No logo, testimonial, case study, or publicity right is granted without separate written permission.

## 13. Fees, suspension, termination, refunds, and survival

Either party may terminate for uncured material breach after **[cure period]** written notice. Immediate suspension/termination may apply to an uncurable security, legal, or prohibited-data risk, with notice where appropriate and lawful. Customer convenience notice remains **[period]**.

**Proposed refund rule for review:** Kach provides a full refund if it cannot provision the agreed pilot or cancels before substantive work begins. After approved customer-data intake or substantive review work begins, the fee is non-refundable except as required by law or agreed in writing. This rule is not approved.

Termination ends access but does not itself erase data. Ownership, confidentiality, payment, reliance limitations, liability, records, Legal Hold, and governing-law provisions survive only for the legally reviewed duration.

## 14. Warranties, liability, and indemnities

Each signatory represents authority to bind its party; Customer represents authority to submit and instruct processing of the data. Otherwise, the pilot and outputs are proposed to be provided “as is” and “as available,” subject to non-excludable rights.

The following require qualified legal review and are intentionally blank:

- monetary liability cap and aggregation rule;
- indirect/consequential damage exclusions;
- privacy, security, confidentiality, infringement, fraud, wilful-misconduct, payment, and indemnity carve-outs;
- Customer-data/misuse indemnity and any Kach IP indemnity; and
- defence control, settlement, mitigation, and cap interaction.

## 15. General terms, precedence, and governing law

The signed Agreement and schedules govern the pilot. For a direct conflict, the signed pilot-specific term should prevail over general website terms, subject to counsel. Amendments, notices, assignment, severability, force majeure, relationship, counterparts, and electronic signature require completed legal wording.

Proposed governing law is Alberta and applicable federal law of Canada. Forum or arbitration is **[pending legal review]**.
