## Recommended first-pilot offer for decision

The recommended offer is a tightly bounded introductory pilot, not a published commitment:

- one invitation-only organization workspace;
- four-week access and review period;
- CAD 199 plus applicable taxes, manually invoiced;
- up to 20 source documents, subject to an approved per-file size/page limit;
- up to two named customer users;
- supported Canadian activity types only where an approved factor and required context exist;
- one reviewed PDF/CSV package, one revision cycle, and one structured feedback session;
- best-effort email support during stated Alberta business hours, with no uptime or response-time SLA;
- AI disabled for real customer data unless `AI-001` is separately approved; and
- customer maintains originals and its authoritative accounting archive.

This recommendation favors a low-friction validation offer. Before approval, the founder should confirm that the manual support and review effort is economically acceptable at CAD 199. If it is not, keep the same four-week/20-document scope and change only the price through an explicit `COM-002` decision.
