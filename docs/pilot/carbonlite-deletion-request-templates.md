## Template controls

- Legal/privacy must approve the final wording, channel, contacts, disclosure limits, and timing.
- Never disclose a confidential Legal Hold reason or imply that a customer request overrides a hold.
- Never promise a contractual deadline based only on the proposed internal targets.
- Never say “permanently deleted,” “all data deleted,” or “backups purged” without independent evidence for every relevant store and exception.
- Do not include source content, credentials, full request messages, or identity documents.
