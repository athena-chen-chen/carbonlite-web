This is the authoritative task list for pilot planning. `DONE` means the linked evidence supports the stated scope. A local or documentation-only completion does not imply production deployment, provider configuration, legal approval, or real-data authorization.

## Status and readiness rules

- Allowed statuses: `NOT_STARTED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `BLOCKED`, `READY_FOR_REVIEW`, `DONE`, `DEFERRED`.
- `Code ready` means the implementation or documentation named by the task is locally reviewable. It does not mean deployed.
- `Production ready` is `NO` until the production change, operational evidence, and required approvals exist.
- `Real-data blocking` is `YES` only when the task must close before real customer data is accepted. Week 1 and later tasks remain conditional on the launch gate but are not themselves pre-launch gates.
- Evidence links identify current evidence or the source of an open requirement. Blank approvals are never inferred as approval.
